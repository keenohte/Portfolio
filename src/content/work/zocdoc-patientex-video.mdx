## Overview

We made a two-minute video that walks through Zocdoc's patient experience end to end, from booking a visit to post-appointment messaging. It was aimed at new providers, and it highlights instant booking, verified reviews, insurance checks, and personalized reminders to build confidence in the marketplace and drive sign-ups. The deliverables included the main video with voiceover, alternate cuts, and motion graphics of the product screens.

## My role

I built the storyboard and designed the original visuals for the video. Working closely with a video editor, I kept the final cut aligned with the goals we set at the start.

## Process

This was a collaborative, iterative build. I worked with stakeholders, the voiceover artist, and the editing team to refine the video across multiple passes, each one tightening how clearly it communicated the value of the experience.

## Results

The video launched on YouTube and was picked up in several blog articles, which widened its reach. It also became the standout in our product-highlight email, driving 66% of all feature clicks, the top performer in the send.

## Reflection

Motion earns its place when a static screen can't carry the feeling of a product. Seeing the flow in sequence, the reminders, the reviews, the instant booking, did more to make Zocdoc feel trustworthy than a list of features ever would.
