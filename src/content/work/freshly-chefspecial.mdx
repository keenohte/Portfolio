## Overview

Chef's Special by Freshly gave customers a way to experience "EATertainment" at home through limited-edition launches from well-known regional and national chefs. The idea paired the pull of destination dining with craveable flavors, without losing Freshly's better-for-you positioning. I designed the brand identity and marketing for the program.

## The idea

The program was a seasonal collaboration that brought four distinct, high-value meals to the Freshly menu, each from a renowned chef. My job was to build a campaign around them that felt as special as the food: a dedicated landing page, email, social, and display, all speaking the same language.

## What it set out to do

The work had a few goals at once. It aimed to raise the perceived value of Freshly meals and keep customers around through higher-quality, chef-driven offerings. It leaned on the chefs' own reputations and networks to earn attention and PR that Freshly couldn't buy outright. And it gave new customers a reason to try Freshly through an exclusive, time-limited experience.

## The campaign

By the end of the year, Freshly had worked with four chefs, each adding a unique, high-value meal to the menu. A visually dynamic landing page anchored everything, uniting the chefs and their dishes in one place. Email, social, and display extended the reach, growing awareness and bringing in new audiences while giving existing customers a reason to stay.

## Reflection

Brand work like this earns its keep when the story is as good as the product. The chefs gave us credibility and reach we couldn't manufacture on our own, and the design's job was to make that feel cohesive from the first banner to the checkout.
