## Overview

Freshly is a direct-to-consumer startup that Nestlé acquired for $1.5B in 2020. As a designer on the team, I helped revitalize its brand identity, working closely with creative leadership to move Freshly from a functional meal-delivery service toward an aspirational lifestyle choice.

## The work

The brand couldn't sit still. My work supported quarterly campaigns, always-on performance marketing, dynamic type and motion, and the launch of new products and sub-brands. That meant designing for the real pace of the business, more than 150 unique SKUs a year, while keeping everything aligned with a refreshed verbal identity so Freshly held together across a crowded market.

## Reflection

Brand at this scale is less about one hero moment and more about consistency under pressure. The interesting problem was keeping a single, recognizable identity intact while shipping constantly across products, campaigns, and channels.
